ANNEX A: SPECIAL PROVISIONS ON PERSONAL GOALS
- A maximum of five (5) active Personal Goal accounts are allowed per customer, each with a due date up to 180 days from creation.
- An active Maya Savings account is required to create a Personal Goal account. No minimum balance is needed, but interest accrues only up to PHP 100,000 per account.
- Goals may be cancelled anytime via the Maya App, moving funds back to Maya Savings.
- Proceeds (deposit plus accrued interest, less taxes) are credited to the Maya Savings account upon cancellation or completion.
- If the linked Maya Savings account becomes dormant, Personal Goal proceeds will not transfer automatically and will not accrue interest until the Savings account is reactivated.

ANNEX B: SPECIAL PROVISIONS ON TIME DEPOSIT PLUS
- A maximum of five (5) active Time Deposit Plus accounts are allowed per customer, with fixed tenors of 3, 6, or 12 months and a non-modifiable maturity date.
- No minimum balance is required, but interest accrues only up to PHP 1,000,000 per account; boosted interest requires a minimum PHP 5,000 target goal.
- Early cancellation is subject to a documentary stamp tax, cancellation fee, and forfeiture of remaining boosted interest (base interest is still credited).
- Proceeds are automatically credited to the Maya Savings account upon cancellation or maturity, and will not transfer automatically if the Savings account has become dormant.

ANNEX C: SPECIAL PROVISIONS ON EXPRESS DEPOSIT (MAYA BLACK EXPRESS CREDIT CARD)
- Express Deposit (ED) is an interest-bearing deposit account opened solely to secure a Maya Black Express (MBE) Credit Card, guaranteed by the ED.
- Under a Deed of Assignment, the ED is irrevocably assigned/held-out to Maya Bank; you may not withdraw or terminate the ED until released, and Maya Bank may debit the hold-out amount to settle outstanding MBE Credit Card obligations upon default.
- The initial credit limit is set at 80% of the ED amount, subject to periodic reassessment by Maya Bank; adding funds to the ED does not automatically increase the credit limit.
- The ED may be cancelled anytime, but funds are released only after thirty (30) days, once all outstanding Credit Card balances are settled.
- Delinquency triggers notification of the outstanding balance, fees, and a payment deadline before the ED is debited; a dispute may be raised within 5-7 business days of the eSOA, during which debiting is suspended if the account remains in good standing.
- Upon final settlement, any remaining ED balance is released within thirty (30) calendar days, net of applicable fees.

ANNEX D: MOBILE CHECK DEPOSIT
- The Mobile Check Deposit Facility allows depositing checks by capturing and transmitting images via the Maya app; the Bank's GT&C and Savings T&C are incorporated by reference.
- Eligibility requires being at least 18 years old, having full legal capacity, and maintaining an active account in good standing; the Bank may approve, deny, suspend, or terminate access at its discretion.
- Eligible checks must be PHP-denominated, drawn on Philippine banks, current-dated (not more than 6 months old), and payable solely to the account holder's name. Checks payable to "Cash," bearing multiple endorsements, photocopied, post-dated, previously processed, altered, or missing essential information are not accepted.
- Checks must be endorsed with "For deposit to Maya Bank," signature, account number, and date, with clear front/back images submitted via the app; blurred, incomplete, or improperly endorsed images may be rejected.
- Deposits are subject to cut-off times and clearing through the Philippine Clearing House Corporation (PCHC), and are not final until cleared. The Bank may impose limits on deposit amount and transaction frequency.
- Customers are responsible for the accuracy, authenticity, and validity of submitted checks and endorsements, and must not resubmit previously dishonored checks or submit checks known/suspected to be fraudulent.
- The original check must be retained by the customer and the Bank keeps a digital copy for at least 5 years; the original must be submitted within 5 calendar days upon request by the Bank, drawee bank, court, or regulator.
- The Bank may reverse or debit deposits for returned, dishonored, fraudulent, or erroneously credited checks, and may debit other accounts to recover amounts if insufficient funds exist.
- Customers agree to indemnify the Bank for losses arising from reliance on submitted check images, fraudulent or defective checks, unauthorized endorsements, or breach of these terms. The Bank is not liable for delays from clearing systems, third parties, or force majeure, nor for indirect or consequential damages.
- The Bank monitors transactions for fraud, may require additional verification, and may disclose check-related information to drawee banks, clearing houses, and regulators as required by law.
